# Glossary

- **Approval**: a manager or owner signs in on the same screen to authorise a sensitive action. See [[roles-and-permissions]].
- **Base currency**: the currency prices are stored in. USD by default.
- **Default currency**: the currency shown on the dashboard, receipts and labels. A setting.
- **Exchange rate**: rate from a currency to the base currency with an effective date. Old rates are kept; each sale and payment stores the rate it used.
- **Internal barcode**: generated by estoq for every variant so any item can be scanned. The one printed on labels.
- **Supplier barcode**: the manufacturer's barcode. Optional, scanned in, never printed.
- **Product**: the parent record with shared details (name, category, brand, images, prices).
- **Variant**: the sellable size and colour combination, with its own SKU, barcodes, price override and stock.
- **Size set**: the sizes offered for a kind of product (letters, numbers, waist and length). One-size items have none.
- **Movement**: one append-only stock ledger row. Stock only changes through movements. Types: opening balance, stock received, sale, customer return, adjustment, void reversal.
- **Needs stock correction**: flag on a variant sold without stock, cleared by a [[stock-adjustments]] entry.
- **Return**: take items back from a settled sale, for a refund or an exchange.
- **Void**: cancel a whole sale on the same day. See [[returns-and-voids]].
- **Recovery key**: shown once at setup; resets owner access and unlocks the encrypted database.
- **Store language**: language of receipts and labels. **App language**: per-user setting.
